Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

"Aan Taen Hai Uploader" (the "App") is a personal tool used by a single creator to send the creator's own short videos to the creator's own TikTok inbox as drafts. It is not offered to the public.

Data the App accesses

  • An OAuth access token for the creator's own TikTok account, used only to upload the creator's own video files as drafts.
  • The upload status of those videos.

How data is used and stored

  • The token is stored only on the creator's own computer and is never shared with any third party.
  • The App does not collect, sell, or share personal data of any other TikTok user.
  • The App does not publish videos. Every video stays a draft until the creator reviews and posts it manually in the TikTok app.

Revoking access

The creator can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites, and the stored token can be deleted from the creator's computer.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest revision.
